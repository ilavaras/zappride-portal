HOW ZAPPRIDE WORKS

Book. Unlock. Ride. Return. Done.

A simple mobile-first journey from a neighbourhood spoke to Chennai One — and back again.

1
📍📱

Find & Book

Open the Zappride app, choose your Hub or Spoke, see available scooters and reserve one.

2
📷🔓

Unlock Vehicle

Reach the parking area, scan the vehicle QR code and unlock the scooter from the app.

3
🛵➡️

Start Journey

The trip starts. GPS and IoT telemetry track location, time, battery and vehicle status.

4
🅿️✅

End Journey

Park inside an approved Hub or Spoke geofence. The app validates the location before closing the ride.

5
₹🧾

Payment & Receipt

The fare is calculated automatically. Payment is processed and the customer receives a trip receipt.
